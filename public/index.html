📖 How to play

⏰ The clock keeps running while this is open!

It's 8:00 PM and your group project is due at 11:59. Finish as many assignments as you can before midnight.

  1. 🎲Get your job. Everyone gets one: 🔎 Research ✍️ Write 🎨 Design ✅ Edit. With fewer than 4 players, some people get two.
  2. 📂Folders land on your desk. Each one shows the steps it needs, in order, and a countdown timer.
  3. 👆Your step? Do it. Tap the colored button and solve a quick task. It takes about 3 seconds.
  4. 📨Not your step? Pass it. Tap the name of a teammate who has that job. The folder flies to their phone.
  5. ⬆️All steps done? Submit it! The sooner you submit, the better the grade.
  6. 🗣️Talk! Say what you're sending and what you need. Teams that talk win.
Tips 🤖 Playing alone? Tap Quick play and bots take the other jobs. 🔴 A red, shaking folder has 5 seconds left. Hurry! 📋 Tap Everyone's assignments to see where every folder is.

🔎 Research

Tap the 2 sources you can actually trust.

✓ Journals, textbooks, government data, expert interviews✗ Anonymous blogs, viral videos, memes, ads, clickbait

✍️ Write

Tap the sentences in the order they belong. Ask yourself what has to happen first, next and last.

🎨 Design

Pick the right chart for what the slide needs to show:

  • Line chart change over time
  • Bar chart comparing amounts
  • Pie chart parts of a whole
  • Map where things are
  • Timeline events in order
  • Flowchart steps of a process
  • Scatter plot how two things relate
  • Venn diagram what overlaps

✅ Edit

Tap the misspelled word. For example, definately should be definitely.

A wrong tap costs you a second and shows a tip, so you learn as you go.

Every so often, something goes wrong. Just like a real group project.

  • 📶Wi-Fi is down One player's screen fills with static for 7 seconds. Everyone else has to cover.
  • 💬Group chat exploded Tap away 3 notifications before you can work again.
  • 🔀Roles swapped Everyone gets a new job. Say yours out loud!
  • 📣Deadline moved up Every folder loses 10 seconds.
  • 🙏Extension granted Every folder gains 12 seconds.
  • 🖨️Printer jam Nobody can submit for 6 seconds.
  • ⏰Last 30 seconds The room turns red. Submit everything you can!

Each folder is graded by how much of its time was left when you submitted it.

  • A Half the time or more left
  • B More than a quarter left
  • C Some time left
  • D Just seconds to spare
  • F Late: the timer ran out

Your group GPA is the average of all grades (A = 4.0 down to F = 0). At 11:59 PM you get a report card with your GPA, a transcript and an award for every player.

Difficulty

  • Sophomore The normal amount of chaos.
  • Junior Tighter deadlines, and work lands on the wrong desk.
  • Senior Finals week. Good luck.
