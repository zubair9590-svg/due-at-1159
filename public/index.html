How to play
  1. Everyone gets a job: 🔎 Research, ✍️ Write, 🎨 Design or ✅ Edit.
  2. Folders land on your desk. If the next step is your job, tap it and solve a 3-second task.
  3. If not, pass it to whoever has that job. Say it out loud!
  4. When every step is done, submit it. Earlier = better grade.
  5. Chaos happens: Wi-Fi drops, deadlines move, roles swap. Adapt.
  6. At 11:59 PM you get a report card with a group GPA and awards.
