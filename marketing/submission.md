## Description (486 of 500 characters)

Due at 11:59 is a real-time party game for 1-8 players where every phone is a different job on the same group project: Research, Write, Design or Edit. Do your quick task, pass the folder to the right teammate, and submit before midnight while chaos hits, like fake Wi-Fi drops and swapped roles. The hard part was keeping every phone in sync. The fun part was racing the rival bots. Playtesting with friends taught us to slow the timers down. Next up: more topics and a classroom mode.
