## Project description

It's 8:00 PM and your group project is due at 11:59. Due at 11:59 is a real-time party game for 1–8 players where every phone becomes a different job on the same project: 🔎 Research, ✍️ Write, 🎨 Design or ✅ Edit.

Assignments land on someone's desk. If the next step is your job, you solve a quick task: pick the sources you can actually trust, put the sentences in order, choose the right chart, or catch the typo. If it isn't your job, you pass the folder to whoever has that job, and it flies from your phone to theirs. Submit before midnight: the earlier you submit, the better the grade.

Then the chaos starts. Someone's Wi-Fi drops and their screen fills with static, the group chat explodes, the professor moves the deadline up, the printer jams, or everyone's roles get swapped. You'll have to talk to each other.

At 11:59 PM your group gets a report card: a GPA, a letter grade, a full transcript and a funny award for every player, from "Carried the Group" to "Last-Minute Legend."

- Join from any phone or laptop with a 4-letter room code or QR code. No login, no install.
- Every screen stays in sync in real time. Players who drop out can rejoin, and their work is shared out meanwhile.
- Bots fill empty seats, so you can play solo or with a small group.
- Four difficulty levels, from Freshman to Senior, and rounds from 2 to 4½ minutes.
- Sound effects, study music and phone vibration.

It's made for students: it turns the most stressful part of school into something you laugh about together, while practicing real skills: checking sources, clear writing, choosing the right visual, and teamwork under pressure.

Try it alone: open the link, tap Create a room, then Quick play with 3 bots.
